PRISM Bridge Privacy Policy

PRISM Bridge is used only by its owner. It accesses only the owner's own Google Drive, solely to copy files between specific Drive folders and the owner's computer.

No data is collected from anyone else, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Google access credentials are stored only on the owner's computer and can be revoked at any time from the owner's Google Account security settings.
